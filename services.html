+ OUR FOCUS

Precision Finishes. Engineered Surfaces.

At Surface Studio, we specialize in premium surface finishing solutions crafted to elevate the visual appeal, durability, and performance of interior and architectural surfaces. From refined PU coatings to ultra-gloss polyester finishes and natural wood treatments, every finish is executed with precision, consistency, and attention to detail.

Our advanced spray booth systems, premium-grade materials, and controlled finishing process allow us to deliver surfaces that feel refined, durable, and visually exceptional. Whether it's residential interiors, commercial environments, luxury furniture, or custom surface applications — we engineer finishes that leave a lasting impression.

PU Polish Services

Designed for modern interiors that demand aesthetics and long-term performance. PU coatings provide a seamless elegant finish with excellent resistance against moisture, scratches, stains, and daily wear. Ideal for modular kitchens, wardrobes, and furniture.

[02]

Polyester Polish Services

Crafted for projects requiring ultra-premium gloss levels with exceptional surface clarity. Known for its mirror-like reflective finish, polyester coating is widely used in luxury interiors and premium furniture applications where superior surface hardness is key.

[03]

Wood Finishing Services

Enhances the natural texture, grain, and richness of wood while protecting it from environmental exposure. We create finishes that retain the authenticity of the material while delivering a refined, durable surface for solid wood, veneers, and interior elements.
