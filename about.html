About Us

Engineered Surfaces.
Crafted with Precision.

Surface Studio is a premium surface finishing studio specializing in advanced coating solutions for modern interiors, luxury furniture, and architectural surfaces. Built on the foundation of precision craftsmanship and engineered finishing systems, we create surfaces that combine durability, refinement, and visual excellence.

From sophisticated PU coatings to ultra-gloss polyester finishes and natural wood treatments, every project is executed with attention to detail, controlled finishing processes, and premium-grade materials.

We believe that a surface is more than just a finish — it is a visual experience that defines the character of a space.

Our Approach

Refined, seamless, and built to last.

At Surface Studio, we focus on delivering finishing solutions that feel refined, seamless, and built to last. Every coating system is carefully selected based on surface type, texture requirement, durability expectations, and aesthetic goals. Our process combines:

Advanced spray booth technology
Professional surface preparation
Precision coating techniques
Controlled finishing environments
Premium polishing systems

This allows us to achieve consistent finish quality with exceptional clarity, texture, and protection.
